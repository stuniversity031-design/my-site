Before you ask

A few common questions.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. Move up when you need more roles covered, or down if you're trimming back. Changes apply at your next billing cycle.

Do I still need any human involvement?

Yes, by design. The system runs the day-to-day work of each role; you still make the calls that need a human — final approvals, big relationship moments, anything with real judgment attached.

What happens after I sign up?

You'll get an onboarding call to map your business to the roles you picked, then setup begins. Starter and Growth are typically running within a week; Scale depends on how many businesses you're configuring.

Is this the same for every business?

No. The roles are the same shape everywhere, but the system is configured against your product, your customers, and your market — not a generic template.
